# Template provenance

The layout was derived from the official TeX source of:

- CAKE: Compiler-Agent Co-Design for Frontier Kernel Evolution
- arXiv:2608.12629v1
- Source: https://arxiv.org/src/2608.12629
- Retrieved: 2026-09-09

The inspected source declares `article` at 11pt, UTF-8/T1 encoding,
US-letter paper with one-inch margins, numbered compressed `natbib` citations,
and `pdflatex` under TeX Live 2025. This archive retains that layout and its
general table, list, and listing conventions.

No CAKE prose, experimental figures, or result tables are included. The title,
abstract, body, diagrams, measurements, and bibliography are specific to the
Runtime Profile Evolution work. The original CAKE source archive is not
vendored here.
